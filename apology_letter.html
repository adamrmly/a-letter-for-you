A personal letter · read whenever you're ready

There are things I should have said more gently.

I made this little page because I wanted to put my thoughts into words carefully, without rushing you to respond.

First, I’m sorry. I’m sorry for the ways I may have hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel that your feelings weren’t being understood. I know that saying “sorry” doesn’t undo anything, and I don’t want to use these words as a way to avoid taking responsibility.

You matter to me, and I’m grateful for the time, care, patience, and memories we’ve shared. Even while I’m struggling to understand my own feelings, I don’t want to dismiss yours or pretend that this is easy.

To be honest, I’m still confused about what I’m feeling and what the right next step should be. I don’t want to make a big decision in a moment of confusion, or promise you something when I’m not yet sure I can give it honestly. I think I need some time and space to understand myself better.

I’m not asking you to wait for me, and I don’t expect you to have an answer right away. You deserve honesty, clarity, and kindness. When you feel ready, I hope we can talk openly about what this means for both of us, and what boundaries would feel fair.

I’m sorry for the hurt this may cause. Whatever happens next, I will respect your feelings and your decision. Thank you for the good you’ve brought into my life. I hope we can speak to each other with care, even while things feel uncertain.

I’m truly sorry. Please take the time you need, too.

One thing I want you to know: You don’t have to reply immediately. I wanted to share this honestly, not pressure you into forgiving me or making a decision before you’re ready.
